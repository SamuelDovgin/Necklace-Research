Complete pendant · English 
Hello, could you quote one custom pendant shipped to Illinois, USA?

Stone: one 2.00–2.05 ct oval IGI-graded lab-grown diamond, D–F, VS1–VS2. Please send two proposed stones with IGI numbers, exact length × width × depth, treatment if reported, and actual videos.

Setting: solid 14K yellow gold (585) throughout, four prongs, a visible top bail, no chain. Please quote solid 18K yellow gold (750) as a separate option. If you have a stock mount that fits the same stone and design, please quote it separately from custom CAD.

Please itemize the stone, net alloy grams excluding the diamond, metal basis/rate, casting/setting/finishing, CAD and revisions, deposit and payment fees, and insured one-piece delivery. Please specify which fees are included, payment currency, quote expiry, expected lead time, weight adjustment and maximum final amount.

Can you make one piece for an individual buyer? Please give your registered Chinese company name, business credit code, invoice/payee name, carrier and service, DAP or DDP with the destination, duties and brokerage treatment, and loss/remake policy. Please quote before taking payment. Thank you.
