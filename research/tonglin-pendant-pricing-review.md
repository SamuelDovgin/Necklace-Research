### Gold weight and price scenarios

The 2.04 ct diamond weighs 0.408 g. If 1.50 g refers to the finished pendant including the stone, roughly **1.092 g** remains after subtracting it. If 1.50 g refers to alloy alone, the combined pendant would weigh roughly **1.908 g**, assuming just those two materials. Ask which quantity the PI uses and how finished metal-only weight will be demonstrated if the pendant is weighed with the stone installed.

## Compare the three ways to buy

1. **Requote the complete Tonglin pendant.** Ask Fannie for the exact stone and one or two lower-priced ovals with videos, plus a finished gold-only weight and a written total for the design. The earlier record describes Fannie as responsive. A written update would resolve whether the itemization and repricing work for this purchase. [Earlier supplier record](records/sourcing.html#v005)
2. **Get one comparable complete quote.** Better Star’s $412 historical offer was for a 2 ct D/VS2 and excludes freight. It is $62.49 below the latest Tonglin pendant subtotal. At the same $52.31 in non-metal setting, labor and CAD charges and $88.66/g, Tonglin would need a stone at **$226.70 or less at 1.50 g**, or **$271.03 or less at 1.00 g**, to match that pre-freight total. Use those as negotiation reference points while getting an updated Better Star quote and complete delivery terms. Qianao’s historical $235 shipped claim remains a lead pending the actual stone, grams, terms and legal seller. ## Reconcile the deposit with the invoice

The earlier record showed two $220 payment requests and one payment processing. At exactly 40%, $220 corresponds to $550. A 40% deposit on $519.49 rounds to $207.80 and on $474.49 to $189.80. If $220 actually settled and is credited in full against $519.49, arithmetic leaves **$299.49** before any weight adjustments or fees. Request the matching PI revision and currency, legal seller and payee, payment receipt with credited amount and any separate fee, status of the second request, and the balance calculation.
