# Tonglin 2.04 ct Oval Pendant — Pricing Problem & Improvement Plan

**Case:** Custom four-prong solitaire pendant with visible bail, solid 14K yellow gold, 2.04 ct E/VS1 IGI-graded lab-grown oval diamond, pendant only (no chain).  
**Source:** Tonglin's November 13, 2026 pro forma invoice (PI) and follow-up discussions with representative Fannie.  
**Status:** Quoted amounts and supplier statements, **not independently verified costs**.

## The problem

The latest total is **$519.49 shipped**, but the pricing is not yet optimized or fully predictable. The diamond is the largest line item, and the **1.50 g “net gold weight” is an estimate** rather than a final, clearly verified finished gold-only measurement. Tonglin says it will reprice using actual gold weight once the pendant is made, but the final formula and a maximum price should be agreed in writing first.

## Where savings might come from

| Lever | Practical improvement | Illustrative opportunity (not promised) |
|---|---|---|
| **Diamond procurement** | Ask for the exact IGI report number; compare the *same stone* at other dealers, then compare similar well-performing 2 ct E–F/VS1–VS2 ovals | ~$30–$100, depending on comparable available inventory |
| **Gold weight** | Obtain CAD-based metal-only weight and design a light but durable basket and bail; confirm actual gold mass excludes diamond | 0.30 g less ≈ **$26.60** saved; 0.50 g less ≈ **$44.33**, if rate unchanged |
| **CAD/wax** | Clarify whether the $20 is for CAD, physical wax, or both; ask about waiver/credit for an approved standard design | Up to $20 |
| **Labor/setting** | Ask whether an existing four-prong casting can meet the specifications; do not compromise secure prongs | Probably limited; combined line is only $32.31 |
| **Shipping** | Compare legitimate, insured DHL/FedEx options and all-in DAP/DDP terms | Possibly modest; do not trade away insurance/compliance |

## Three sourcing approaches

1. **Optimize Tonglin's complete pendant (simplest).** Keep one responsible manufacturer. Request the exact IGI diamond, CAD gold-only weight, fixed or capped cost formula, and an itemized revised PI before production.
2. **Benchmark matched complete quotes.** Ask Better Star, Provence/Qianao, and other vendors to price the *same* diamond grade, gold purity, visible-bail CAD, and shipping basis. Do **not** compare prices without verifying whether the chain, certification, import charges, or actual gold weight are included.
3. **Split diamond and mounting sourcing (more involved).** Get a loose-stone quote and an empty 14K four-prong mounting quote, then price setting and shipping separately. Use this only if one party agrees in writing to take responsibility for fit, breakage during setting, finished quality, and delivery.

## Questions to resolve before paying the balance

- [ ] What is the **IGI report number** of the exact 2.04 ct diamond, and can I view its video and report?
- [ ] Does **1.50 g** mean **gold alone**, excluding the 0.408 g diamond and any chain?
- [ ] What is the **CAD-estimated gold-only weight**? What are the bail and prong dimensions and permitted revisions?
- [ ] What **per-gram gold rate** will determine the final price? Does the invoice decrease *and* increase with actual gold usage, and is there a maximum approved total?
- [ ] Can Tonglin issue a PI that specifically states **solid 14K (585), not plated**, the IGI report number, approved design, delivery terms, and remedies for defects/non-delivery?
- [ ] Does the **$45 shipping** include insurance and which import fees remain payable? Is shipping of finished diamond jewelry approved by the carrier?
- [ ] How does the **$220 deposit** reconcile to the signed PI and the stated 40% deposit rule? ($220 corresponds to 40% of $550, not $519.49.)

## Decision rule

**Choose the lowest verifiable *delivered* price for the actual approved stone and durable finished design, not the lowest advertised number.** Diamond sourcing is probably the largest dollar opportunity; gold-only weight is the next. Protect quality and payment terms rather than shaving a few dollars from the small labor/setting lines.

**Hypothetical example, not an offer:** Keeping all non-gold charges and $45 shipping fixed, a $225 diamond and **1.00 g** of gold at $88.66/g would yield approximately **$411.97 shipped** (`225 + 88.66 + 4.62 + 27.69 + 20 + 45`). This does **not** establish that such a stone or weight is available or acceptable.

Ask Fannie for **(1) the exact IGI report, (2) CAD gold-only weight and minimum safe dimensions, and (3) a written gold-adjustment formula and maximum final total**. Request one matched competing quote before committing to the balance.
