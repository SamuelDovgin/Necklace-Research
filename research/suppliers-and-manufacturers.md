## Find suppliers for a new piece

1. Write down the exact piece, metal and finish, stone size and shape, minimum order, target dimensions, and acceptable substitutes.
2. Search the supplier directory and switch to the relevant product or quote register. Use completed-purchase reports to identify who made a similar piece. Use catalogs for discovery and screening.
3. Issue the same brief to the shortlisted companies and ask each to itemize the main stone, alloy-only grams, chain, CAD, shipping, and actual seller/payee. Preserve the source currency and delivery terms.
4. Record your response to each reply, the evidence behind it, and what would change the decision in the [journal](journal.md). The sourcing dossier also has a [district-by-district prospecting brief](records/sourcing.html#16-future-research-brief-find-chinese-jewelry-district-suppliers-beyond-alibaba) for a future search beyond the marketplace leads.

## What to record when researching a company

- Legal name if available, storefront, contact channel, and date checked. Record any uncertainty about aliases or which company issued a quote.
- Who supplies the stone, who manufactures or sets the piece, and who handles inspection, warranty, and repairs. Capture the evidence for each role.
- Relevant catalog pages, prior order examples, stock versus custom work, minimum order, lead time, and quote terms.
- How stone selection, CAD approval, changes, payment, and pre-shipment quality checks are handled.
- What to ask next and where the supporting reply or file is saved.
