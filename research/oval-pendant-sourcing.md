## What I liked and what gave me pause

- **Tonglin:** The buyer found Fannie fast and helpful, and valued hearing the CAD and production milestones. Sofia’s reply left the earlier 1.50 g estimate ambiguous. For the Nov 11 proposal, if that is total pendant mass, subtracting the 0.404 g stone gives about 1.096 g of remaining material. If it is gold-only, the total would be about 1.904 g. The archived $220 payment appeared to be processing. The [Nov 13 review](tonglin-pendant-pricing-review.md) calculates weight scenarios for the newer stone and reconciles possible deposit amounts.
- **Better Star:** Jerry’s quick answer about estimated ~1 g gold and timing made the offer easy to scope. The actual stone, final repricing, and freight remain open. Better Star and StarsGem are different companies.
- **LGG:** Lynn provided photos quickly. The correction after the buyer chose 2 ct undermined the original price comparison. Keep both messages as dated records, with the old 2 ct figure marked superseded.
- **Hanchen / PDM:** Quick replies and specific price alternatives were helpful. Hanchen’s chain terms and relationship to the separately recorded Good Century contact remain open. PDM’s technical CAD and weight follow-up had no captured answer.
- **Provence / Qianao:** The quoted prices and legal seller strings are materially different. Keep Qianao’s claimed affiliation in the notes and verify the order-specific seller, rep, and payee before relying on it.
