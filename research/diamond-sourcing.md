## What to record for a candidate

- Supplier and exact stock ID, listing link or inventory row, date checked, currency, and whether the price covers a loose stone or a setting.
- Shape, carat weight, measurements, color, clarity, growth method if disclosed, and the grading report number. Record supplier-stated details and your verification result.
- Face-up photos, a video of the actual stone, and visual preferences. For pears, compare outline, length-to-width ratio, and appearance in motion alongside price.
- Open questions, next check, and the dated [journal entry](journal.md) where the candidate was studied.
