## Record the terms of an offer

1. Save the seller, SKU, date, original currency, price tier or discount, quote expiry, and whether it is a listing or a written quote.
2. Match what is included. Record stone and grading report, metal and finished weight, chain and clasp, CAD, and any custom work.
3. Record the exchange rate and date used. Add any quoted shipping, payment fees, and estimated duties or taxes to estimate landed cost. Keep unknown fees visible.
4. Record which terms affect value, including returns, warranty, substitutions, lead times, and quality checks.
5. Write down the comparable offer, your decision, and what would change it in the [research journal](journal.md).
