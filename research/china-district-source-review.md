## Claims that changed the shortlist

- **CN-004 Jinxiuxiu.** The directory matches the name, number and K-gold/platinum categories. Its foreign-trade sentence is a general invitation for exporting businesses to add an email. It did not substantiate this seller’s export, custom CAD or lab-diamond capabilities. Ask first about the specific service you need. [Directory record](https://www.aichangjia.com/factory/45679.html)
- **Panyu association rows.** Most original directory pages failed direct HTTPS retrieval, with a hostname mismatch on the local check. Indexed exact-name results corroborate Yingde, Zhengjin, Yiheng and Kaishaqi. A cached result also matches Huiju’s business phone and gold/silver setting specialty. The other numbers and terms remain archived leads. - **CN-005 Shuibei Jeweler.** The indexed contact matches the workbook. Its FAQ gives product-dependent MOQ. - **CN-003 Wuzhou Provence Gems and CN-041 Qianao.** The directory supports the named moissanite business in Wuzhou. Qianao has its own platform identity and an archived $235 seller claim. ## Decide whether a split order saves money

Better Star’s **$412** is the strongest lower like-for-like pre-freight anchor in the workbook, conditional on confirming what was offered. Qianao’s **$235 shipped** is an unverified price claim. The smaller StarsGem and Provence stones, the $695 Hanchen chain bundle, and the PDM offers with unspecified metal require different comparisons. For Better Star and LGG, the district workbook dates differ from entries in the earlier archive. Use the seller’s dated written revision for the next quote. [District quote anchors](supplier-directory.html?view=district-prices) · [Earlier quote register](supplier-directory.html?view=quotes)

The wholesale $185.84 figure is a trade estimate. Public retail examples do not establish a cheaper loose-stone purchase. A 2.00 ct E/VS2 Veyara stone advertised at $692 during review, and its other comparable example at $480 was already sold. The Loose Grown Diamond example indexed at $552 returned 404 on recheck. Seek a live, exact-stone direct quote from Changsheng or Better Star before assuming a split saves money. If you go split, compare one domestic handoff and one insured export against a US bench quote. ## Close the quote with a comparable total

Ask who actually casts and sets the piece, who invoices, and who takes responsibility for the exact stone during the build. Obtain written 585 or 750 designation, the exact stone report and video, CAD and gold-weight adjustment, local handoff or carrier service, DAP/DDP terms or equivalent breakdown, insurance and claim terms, and a quote expiry. Enter unknown charges as open until confirmed. Keep the final invoice tied to the exact stone and approved CAD.

CBP’s low-value and jewelry pages establish why an all-in price requires an item-specific customs estimate. Carrier guidance also distinguishes general service claims from acceptance of a particular route/account. Confirm carrier eligibility and insured value from the shipper’s written terms, including for a domestic handoff followed by export.
