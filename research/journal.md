## Adding an entry

Add each research session near the top of this file, newest first. Use the date the work happened and a descriptive title. The three newest entries appear on the hub automatically. Give each entry a short plain-text first paragraph for the preview, then link to the detailed note, quote, listing capture, or source file. Record changes of mind in a new entry and link back to the earlier decision.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — What I worked on

One sentence on what I looked at or what changed.

- **Question:** What was I trying to find out?
- **Evidence:** Which listing, report, quote, file, or conversation did I use?
- **Finding:** What did I learn, and which details are seller-stated or inferred?
- **Decision:** What did I shortlist, rule out, or change my mind about?
- **Still open:** What would I check next?
```
