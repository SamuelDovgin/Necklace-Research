Wholesale, export, and retail context

  • Wholesale trend

    The published 2 ct E–F/VS fancy benchmark was $105/ct in Q2 2025, $82/ct in Q2 2026, and $92/ct in Q3 2026. That last move was +12.2% quarter over quarter for this cell. Golan’s long-run index was 93% below July 2018, with a mid-2024 change from manufacturer parcels to smaller transactions in certified stones.
