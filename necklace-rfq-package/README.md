# Necklace RFQ Research Package

Prepared: 2026-07-07

This package is for sourcing a solid 14k yellow gold two-stone lab diamond necklace inspired by the StarsGem reference design.

## Start here

1. Read [docs/01_reference_spec.md](docs/01_reference_spec.md) and adjust any preferences, especially chain length, clasp, IGI requirement, and budget ceiling.
2. Compare the five live StarsGem options—including CNY/USD conversion—in [docs/07_starsgem_listing_comparison.md](docs/07_starsgem_listing_comparison.md).
3. Use [docs/08_gold_alloy_color_guide.md](docs/08_gold_alloy_color_guide.md) to choose 14K vs 18K and yellow vs white gold.
4. Send the message in [docs/03_rfq_message_templates.md](docs/03_rfq_message_templates.md) to the first-wave suppliers: Tianyu, Paston, Fiorese, LGG, Provence, and StarsGem.
5. Track replies in [outputs/necklace-rfq/Necklace_RFQ_Tracker.xlsx](outputs/necklace-rfq/Necklace_RFQ_Tracker.xlsx).
6. Use [dashboard/index.html](dashboard/index.html) as the editable research library. It contains the overall product specification, Alibaba comparison sheet, master supplier guide, and one independently editable/exportable Markdown page for each ranked supplier.
7. Do not pay a deposit until the order contract, CAD/CAD timing, stone sourcing process, and certificate language are written down.

## Current supplier ranking for this necklace

1. StarsGem - exact design and strongest price evidence.
2. Provence Gems - established Alibaba alternative and heavier/IGI benchmark.
3. Tianyu Gems - quality and CAD baseline.
4. Anster Fine Jewelry - aggressive Alibaba price challenger requiring exact variant proof.
5. Shenzhen Hong Yun Fu - second Alibaba price check.
6. LGG Jewelry - closest alternate two-stone architecture.
7. Fiorese Jewelry - stone/certification and chain-gauge baseline.
8. Paston Jewelry - Alibaba custom backup.
9. Kuololit - catalog backup.
10. Messi Gems - loose-stone/factory backup.
11. Mona Fine Jewelry - personal-service backup.
12. Ouros / Golden Bird - India-based comparison.

## Files

- `docs/` - Markdown research and sourcing workflow.
- `docs/07_starsgem_listing_comparison.md` - five-listing StarsGem specification, certificate, construction, and currency comparison.
- `docs/08_gold_alloy_color_guide.md` - short 14K/18K and yellow/white gold decision guide.
- `data/` - editable CSV source tables.
- `data/starsgem_listings.csv` - machine-readable five-listing specifications and currency calculations.
- `dashboard/` - local Markdown research library with browser autosave, ranked supplier pages, source images, search, and per-page `.md` export.
- `outputs/necklace-rfq/Necklace_RFQ_Tracker.xlsx` - main workbook with scoring formulas.

## Source posture

Reddit/community sentiment is useful as a screening signal, not proof of current quality. The purchase decision should be based on written supplier answers, comparable quotes, CAD, stone videos, certificate verification, and pre-shipment QC.
