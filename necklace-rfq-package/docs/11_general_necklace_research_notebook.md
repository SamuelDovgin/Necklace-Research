## 5. Preference tests

Questions to answer through comparison rather than intuition:

- [ ] Solitaire pear or round-over-pear drop?
- [ ] Elongated pear around 1.65–1.70 L/W or fuller pear around 1.50–1.55?
- [ ] Yellow gold, white gold, or compare both using the same CAD?
- [ ] 14K durability/value or 18K color/premium?
- [ ] Approximately 1 ct pear or a visibly larger 1.5–2.3 ct pear?
- [ ] Fixed pendant or articulated connection?
- [ ] Barely-there chain or a measured minimum width/weight?
- [ ] IGI-required or exact-stone video plus written specs sufficient?
- [ ] Stock piece, modified stock design, or fully custom build?

## 6. Comparison rules

Do not compare headline prices until these are normalized:

1. Finished necklace versus pendant only.
2. 10K, 14K, and 18K gold.
3. Yellow gold versus rhodium-plated white gold.
4. Total carat weight and each stone's individual size.
5. Gross weight versus inferred non-stone mass.
6. Chain length, dimensions, clasp, and chain-only weight.
7. Certified versus merely supplier-graded stones.
8. CNY price, StarsGem USD conversion, shipping, duties, and payment fees.
9. Stock inventory versus made-to-order customization.
10. Written QC/remake protection and exact-stone identity.
