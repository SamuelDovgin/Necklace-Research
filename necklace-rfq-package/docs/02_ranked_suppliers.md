# 02 - Ranked Supplier List

This ranking is tuned for a custom 14k yellow gold lab diamond necklace, not loose stones alone.

| Rank | Supplier | Role | Why quote them | Risk control |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tianyu Gems | Quality baseline | Custom 14k/18k fine jewelry when cut quality, CAD discipline, and finish matter. | Can cost more than budget factories; confirm chain weight and pear video before deposit. |
| 2 | Paston Jewelry | Best Alibaba quote candidate | Apples-to-apples Alibaba RFQ with Trade Assurance, custom necklace build, and written specs. | Get stone details in writing; do not accept generic certificate images. |
| 3 | Fiorese Jewelry | Certification/transparency baseline | Lab-diamond-forward custom work where IGI report numbers and stone control are important. | Potentially higher quote; make sure payment protection terms are explicit. |
| 4 | LGG Jewelry / Igg Jewelry | Lab-diamond-specific candidate | Quote candidate if you want lab diamond sourcing to be central rather than an add-on. | Less price history in this exact necklace category; verify channel, terms, and QC process. |
| 5 | Provence Gems | Price pressure / volume benchmark | Competitive quote to test the floor price for a 14k custom lab diamond pendant. | Mixed community sentiment; use tight written specs, CAD signoff, and pre-shipment checks. |
| 6 | StarsGem | Reference design / price baseline | Baseline for the exact visual design; useful if they can customize certificate and chain specs. | The supplied listing text says none certified; do not assume IGI unless they provide a report number. |
| 7 | Mona Fine Jewelry | Personal-service backup | More individual custom workflow if you value rep taste and handholding over Alibaba process. | Harder to compare factory-level terms; buyer protection and timeline need extra clarity. |
| 8 | Kuololit | Budget backup | Simple stock-style 14k pieces; only use for this if quote is much cheaper and specs are nailed down. | Less natural fit for a custom IGI pear pendant; communication/QC can be more variable. |
| 9 | Messi Gems | Optional comparison | Maybe quote only if first-wave options disappoint or you need another Wuzhou price point. | Community evidence is thinner and includes serious certificate/stone-substitution concerns; verify hard. |
| 10 | Ouros / Goldenbird | Not first-wave | Use only if you already like a specific listing or want an India-based comparison. | Recent community caution signals mean it should not be the first choice for a sentimental custom piece. |

## Narrow first wave

Send the same RFQ to Tianyu, Paston, Fiorese, LGG, Provence, and StarsGem first. Keep the other four as backups or caution/watchlist entries.

## How to interpret sentiment

- 4.0-5.0: strong enough to quote early, assuming written terms are clear.
- 3.0-3.9: real candidate but needs extra QC and buyer-protection checks.
- Under 3.0: quote only for comparison or if you have a specific reason.
